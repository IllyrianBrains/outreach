# What do protesters want?

The movement's website distinguishes **initial demands** from a later, broader platform. These are political positions of the movement, not the neutral requests in our international outreach letters.

## The movement's demands

The movement calls for:

- the government's resignation;
- reversal of rules concerning strategic investors and the *Mountains Package*; and
- reversal of changes to protected-area and cultural-heritage laws.

Its later platform also proposes a 12-month nonpartisan transitional government, constitutional and electoral reform, changes to party financing, limits on prime-ministerial terms and wider civic consultation.

These are the movement's positions. They should not be presented as views shared by every protest participant.

## What this site's letters ask for

The outreach letters make narrower requests: protect peaceful protesters and journalists, ensure medical and legal access, preserve evidence, investigate allegations impartially and support dialogue. A sender does not have to endorse the movement's political platform.
