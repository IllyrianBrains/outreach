# Movement context

This page summarizes public accounts of the Flamingo Revolution. It does not imply that every participant supports every published position.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-account-group-outline: **Who is involved**

    The Flamingo Revolution describes itself as a decentralized civic movement involving protesters in Albania, students and Albanian diaspora communities.

    Reporting also includes journalists, bystanders, police, private security, public authorities and civil-society organizations. Their actions and claims should be assessed separately.

-   :material-format-list-checks: **What the movement demands**

    The movement began with opposition to development affecting the Vjosë–Nartë protected coastal landscape. Its published demands include the government’s resignation and reversal of rules concerning strategic investors, the *Mountains Package*, protected areas and cultural heritage.

    Its later platform also proposes political and electoral changes. These are the movement’s positions, not the neutral requests made in this site’s outreach letters.

-   :material-map-marker-radius-outline: **Where protests were reported**

    The earliest reported protests took place around Akërni, Narta Lagoon and Zvërnec. Demonstrations later spread to Tirana, other Albanian cities and Albanian diaspora communities abroad.
