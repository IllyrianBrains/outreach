# Funding and support

No consolidated public report was found showing the recipient, total collected and spending for movement-wide crowdfunding.

- **[PPNEA annual reports · annual archive; checked 18 October 2026 ↗](https://ppnea.org/annual-reviews/?lang=en)**  
  The organization’s archive of annual activity and disclosure reports. These records can identify project support, but they are not a consolidated account of civic-movement donations.

- **[EcoAlbania annual report · 2025 reporting year ↗](https://ecoalbania.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Eco-Albania-Anual-Report-2025-Web.pdf)**  
  A report on the organization’s conservation, community and cultural work, including its own activities and partnerships. Organizational funding should not automatically be attributed to a particular protest or referendum.

- **[EuroNatur annual report · 2024 reporting year ↗](https://www.euronatur.org/fileadmin/docs/Publikationen/Geschaeftsbericht/EuroNatur_Annual_Report_2024_web.pdf)**  
  An organizational report identifying conservation projects, partners and funding. It documents EuroNatur’s work, not a movement-wide Flamingo Revolution account.

- **[Patagonia conservation support · 19 March 2024 ↗](https://www.patagoniaworks.com/press/2024/3/19/no-one-said-nature-protection-is-easy)**  
  An account of longer-term support for grassroots conservation in the Vjosa campaign. This predates the 2026 mobilization and should be treated as separate conservation support unless a direct link is documented.

These conservation grants should not be described as funding the Flamingo Revolution without direct evidence. A donation link alone is not a financial report.
