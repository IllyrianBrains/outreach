# Moratorium

Campaigners called for a pause on new permits and irreversible development in protected areas while legal and environmental questions are reviewed.

- **[European Parliament procedure and adopted text · 17 June 2026 ↗](https://oeil.europarl.europa.eu/oeil/en/procedure-file?reference=2025%2F2250%28INI%29)**  
  The official record of Parliament’s position supporting a moratorium on new permits and construction in protected areas while incompatible legal provisions are addressed. It is a political and accession-related position, not an Albanian stop-work order.

- **[European Parliament outcome — BirdLife · 17 June 2026 ↗](https://www.birdlife.org/news/2026/06/17/press-release-european-parliament-backs-nature-as-albanias-flamingo-revolution-reaches-strasbourg/)**  
  A civil-society explanation of the environmental provisions adopted by Parliament and their relationship to the Vjosë–Nartë campaign.

- **[Vjosa Delta updates — EuroNatur · 11–12 June 2026 ↗](https://www.euronatur.org/en/what-we-do/news/alarming-developments-in-the-narta-lagoon)**  
  Dated updates about construction, public pressure and a reported temporary suspension at one site. A site-level suspension should not be read as evidence of a nationwide moratorium.

- **[Environment Ministry protest — PPNEA · 26 May 2026 ↗](https://ppnea.org/njoftim-per-shtyp-ndaloni-masakren-ne-vjose-narte/)**  
  A civil-society statement about a protest outside the Environment Ministry calling for an immediate halt to interventions in Pishë Poro–Nartë and an institutional response.

The European Parliament position is not an Albanian stop-work order, and a temporary site suspension is not a national moratorium.
