# Civic projects

Selected public tools and volunteer initiatives associated with the 2026 mobilization.

- **[Your Ideas · active by 18 October 2026; launch date not stated ↗](https://www.flamingorevolution.eu/idete-tuaja/)**  
  A public form for submitting proposals and civic concerns that may continue beyond individual protests. Submitted ideas can be displayed and assessed by other visitors.

- **[Draft Laws · active by 18 October 2026; launch date not stated ↗](https://www.flamingorevolution.eu/projektligje/)**  
  Proposed legal texts published for public reading, download and discussion. They are civic drafts, not enacted legislation.

- **[Flamingo Dossier · active by 18 October 2026; launch date not stated ↗](https://www.flamingorevolution.eu/dosjet/)**  
  A collection of public-record dossiers about institutions, people and disputed issues. Readers should follow the cited records and distinguish documented facts from allegations.

- **[Protest Map · active by 18 October 2026; launch date not stated ↗](https://www.flamingorevolution.eu/harta/)**  
  A map recording where and when demonstrations were reported in Albania and diaspora cities. It is a movement-maintained chronology rather than an independently verified census.

- **[Protest Pulse · active by 18 October 2026; launch date not stated ↗](https://www.flamingorevolution.eu/pulsi-protestes/)**  
  Participation estimates over time accompanied by the publisher’s methodology. Crowd figures remain the project’s own estimates unless corroborated independently.

- **[Diaspora Zbarkon · active by 18 October 2026; launch date not stated ↗](https://www.diaspora-zbarkon.com/en)**  
  Information about diaspora marches, routes and coordination for Albanians travelling or organizing abroad. Event details should be rechecked before travel because routes and times may change.

- **[Flamingo Times · September 2026 ↗](https://www.flamingorevolution.eu/flamingo-times/)**  
  A downloadable and printable movement newspaper covering civic demands and public issues. Its articles reflect the publisher’s perspective and should be attributed accordingly.

- **[Referendum 21 · collection began 24 August 2026 ↗](https://www.flamingorevolution.eu/referendum/)**  
  Information, legal context and participation resources for the initiative seeking repeal of the 2024 Protected Areas amendments. The page supports the campaign but is not the official KQZ record.

- **[Albanian Diaspora Civic Network · dossier submitted 13 July 2026 ↗](https://albaniandiasporacivicnetwork.com/en/our-work/)**  
  Volunteer work in policy, research, civic participation and public information. Its documented work includes a Vjosë–Nartë evidence dossier submitted to the European Parliament’s ENVI Committee.

These are separate initiatives, and their publications do not represent every protest participant.
