# Urgent evidence update — Tirana protests, 13–15 October 2026

**Updated:** 15 October 2026  
**Status:** Developing; not an independent investigation  
**Audience:** Newsrooms, diplomatic missions, elected representatives, civil society, international monitoring bodies

!!! warning "Time-sensitive: safety and evidence preservation"
    Reports of injuries, detention and interference with journalists warrant prompt, impartial scrutiny. Figures and circumstances may change. Do not interpret reported allegations as established findings.

## Executive summary

Student-led demonstrations in Tirana beginning 13 October have continued through 15 October amid clashes. Reporting describes police use of tear gas and water cannon, objects thrown at police, fires and property damage. Accounts include injuries among protesters, police officers, media workers and bystanders. Authorities have an obligation to protect peaceful assembly and journalists, while investigating violence by any participant.

## Immediate requests to competent authorities

1. Protect peaceful demonstrators, students, minors, journalists, bystanders, detainees and police officers from harm.
2. Preserve body-camera and CCTV footage, command logs, deployment orders, custody records, incident reports and relevant medical documentation in accordance with privacy and evidentiary rules.
3. Conduct prompt, independent, person-specific inquiries into serious injuries, alleged excessive force, attacks on journalists and violence committed by protesters or others.
4. Ensure detainees' access to lawyers, family notification and necessary medical attention; publish aggregate, appropriately anonymized detention and injury information.
5. Communicate publicly about investigative responsibility, procedural safeguards and timelines without prejudging outcomes.

## Open questions

- What are the verified circumstances and medical findings in the serious leg-injury case?
- Which specific force deployments were ordered, by whom and with what warnings?
- What evidence is available for each alleged assault on a journalist or media worker?
- What oversight investigations are open, and how will their independence be ensured?
- How many people were detained or treated each day, using consistent definitions?
