# Questions international readers may ask

**Is this only an environmental protest?** It began with protected-area development disputes, according to the movement's chronology, but later encompassed broader governance and socioeconomic concerns.

**Is the movement a political party?** Its website describes a decentralized civic initiative. Do not infer that every participant agrees with every published demand.

**What about violence at protests?** Some published accounts describe violence by individual participants as well as allegations of excessive police force. Each incident needs individual, impartial investigation. The right of peaceful assembly is not automatically extinguished by the actions of others.

**How reliable are the numbers?** Protest counts and crowd sizes are estimates unless supported by a stated methodology and independent evidence. Attribute them to the publisher.

**How can I help without taking a political side?** Share verified reporting, request protection of fundamental rights, ask for independent investigations, and seek dialogue.

**What evidence is available for 13 October?** See the [ADCN public-source brief](../assets/ADCN_Tirana_13_October_2026_Evidence_Brief.pdf) and [source directory](../directory.md). The brief explicitly distinguishes images, reporting and unresolved claims.
