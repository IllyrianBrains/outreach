# How can people respond?

You can support fundamental rights without endorsing a party or every demand of the movement.

1. **Check the evidence.** Confirm the date, place, source and context.
2. **Choose a recipient.** Find media, public representatives or civil-society groups in your country.
3. **Send a short message.** Ask for protection of peaceful assembly and journalists, impartial investigation of alleged violations and constructive dialogue.
4. **Protect people.** Do not expose private information or share graphic material without a clear public-interest reason.
5. **Follow up respectfully.** Keep a record of replies and correct errors openly.
