# Share a documented briefing

1. **Choose your country** from the Countries tab.
2. **Choose a recipient:** journalist, elected representative, human-rights organization or diplomatic institution.
3. **Copy the letter** in the appropriate language. Replace the name, location and institution; keep claims attributed to their sources.
4. **Attach the evidence brief** and include the [source index](sources.md). Say clearly that the PDF covers 13 October only.
5. **Follow up politely** and record any answer. Do not send bulk messages to private addresses or imply affiliation with ADCN without authorization.
