# Who is involved?

The **Flamingo Revolution** describes itself as a decentralized civic movement. Its public activities involve protesters in Albania, students and members of Albanian diaspora communities.

Other people and institutions appear in reporting about the protests, including journalists, bystanders, police, private security, public authorities and civil-society organizations. Their actions and claims should be assessed separately.

This outreach guide is maintained by **Illyrian Brains**. It provides public information and letter templates; it does not claim that every participant shares the same political position.
