## Verification standard

!!! tip "How sources are checked"
    Keep the original publisher, URL, publication date and event date. Distinguish original records, attributed reports, allegations and verified findings. Check the origin, completeness, date and location of images or footage; attribute counts and quotations; seek relevant responses; protect private or vulnerable people; and record material corrections openly.

Avoid describing an entire assembly as violent or peaceful from isolated footage. Do not treat a statement, arrest or disciplinary action as proof of guilt.
