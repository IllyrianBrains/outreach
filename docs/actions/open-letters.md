# Open letters

Published public appeals and statements, listed newest first.

- **[Students and protesters — Diaspora Akademike, 18 October 2026 ↗](https://diaspora-akademike.org/#studentet-en)**  
  Calls for education reform, youth opportunities, democratic protections and review of reported uses of force.

- **[Letter to EU leaders — Diaspora Akademike, 3 July 2026 ↗](https://diaspora-akademike.org/#eu-en)**  
  Requests EU attention to education reform, academic independence and research.

- **[Support for civic protests — Diaspora Akademike, 17 June 2026 ↗](https://diaspora-akademike.org/#deklarata-en)**  
  Supports peaceful protest and calls for stronger education and accountable institutions.

- **[Protected Areas statement — environmental organizations, 12 June 2026 ↗](https://www.euronatur.org/en/what-we-do/news/alarming-developments-in-the-narta-lagoon)**  
  Rejects official consultation until disputed legal changes are repealed.

- **[UK Albanian community open letter — May 2026 ↗](https://ukapfoundation.org/open-letter-i)**  
  Requests transparency, independent review and consultation on major development projects.

- **[Sazan Island concern letter — 22 January 2026 (PDF) ↗](https://ppnea.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Concern-Letter_Sazan-Island-Case_Final.pdf)**  
  Calls for suspension of the resort project and stronger protection for the island.

- **[Vlorë International Airport letter — 15 September 2020 ↗](https://www.albrafting.org/blog/open-letter-national-and-international-environmental-civil-society-organizations-addressed)**  
  Calls on the government to cancel airport plans in the Vjosë–Nartë Protected Area.
