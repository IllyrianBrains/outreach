# Country routing guide

Use this guide to turn the outreach templates into a country-specific message. It covers the 21 countries in the Flamingo Revolution network. Links point to institutional entry pages rather than personal addresses so that senders can confirm the current office-holder and public contact channel before sending.

!!! warning "Targeted outreach, not a mass mailing"
    Send only to institutions whose mandate fits the request. Use a public institutional channel, identify your connection to the country truthfully, and keep a record of the source, date, recipient and response. Do not copy every institution into one email.

## How to route a message

1. Choose the sender's country of residence or strongest legitimate connection.
2. Start with **one primary public authority**: the foreign ministry, the country's diplomatic mission responsible for Albania, or the sender's elected representative.
3. Add **one specialist recipient** only when relevant: a parliamentary committee, human-rights body, journalist organization or newsroom.
4. For EU countries, ask for bilateral action and for the issue to be raised through EU institutions. For Council of Europe, OSCE or NATO members, ask only for action within the recipient's actual mandate.
5. Adapt the salutation and the request; do not imply that an institution has investigative authority in Albania when it does not.

## Recipient choice by template

| Template | Best first recipient | Useful secondary recipient |
|---|---|---|
| Foreign representative | Your constituency representative or the relevant parliamentary committee | Foreign ministry or diplomatic mission responsible for Albania |
| Media pitch | A named foreign/news editor, Balkans correspondent or investigations desk | Journalist union or press-freedom organization when the pitch concerns attacks on media workers |
| Civil society / institutional request | National human-rights institution, ombudsman or NGO with a relevant mandate | Foreign ministry human-rights desk, diplomatic mission or specialist parliamentary committee |

## Minimum country adaptation

Replace every bracketed field before sending:

- `[COUNTRY]`, `[CITY/REGION]` and `[LANGUAGE]`;
- the exact institution, office and public contact channel;
- the sender's truthful connection: constituent, resident, citizen, diaspora member, journalist or organization;
- the specific action that office can take; and
- the evidence-brief link and the date it was last checked.

When writing from Kosovo or Albania, avoid describing the sender as part of a “foreign” constituency if that is inaccurate. When writing to a non-resident embassy, address the mission actually accredited to Albania and confirm its remit on the linked official directory.
