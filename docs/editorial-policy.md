# About this project and documentation approach

**Publisher:** Illyrian Brains. **Purpose:** Support human rights protection, public understanding and responsible communication about protests in Albania.

This website is **not an official Flamingo Revolution website** and does not speak for protest organizers, ADCN, the Albanian authorities or any other organization. Linking to a source does not imply endorsement. This resource is not a finding of legal liability.

## Editorial commitments

1. **Human rights for everyone.** Peaceful assembly, freedom of expression, due process, physical safety and the rights of journalists apply regardless of political affiliation. Violence against protesters, bystanders, police or journalists should be investigated impartially.
2. **Separate claims and findings.** Distinguish what images show, what witnesses allege, what publishers report, what institutions state and what independent investigations establish. Avoid declaring a crime, motive or responsibility without sufficient evidence.
3. **Multiple perspectives.** Include relevant official responses, credible independent reporting and civil-society statements. The movement's history and demands are attributed to the movement, not presented as independently established facts.
4. **Source traceability.** Maintain the CSV [source directory](directory.md), [incident registry](incidents.md) and [evidence gallery](evidence-gallery.md) with source IDs, dates and review status. A report of an incident is not proof of its cause.
5. **Safety and privacy.** Do not expose witness identities, minors' personal details, private medical records or identifiable vulnerable people without a clear lawful basis and safeguards. Link to third-party photographs rather than republishing without permission.
6. **Corrections.** Record updates and corrections with dates; never silently change material facts in published incident records.
7. **Nonpartisan public-interest focus.** No party endorsements, threats, harassment or collective blame. Requests to authorities should seek safeguards, dialogue, transparent procedures and impartial investigations rather than predetermined political outcomes.

## Corrections and contributions

When proposing a new source, include its original URL, publisher, publication date, event date if known, a neutral description and verification status. For an incident, supply a separate source reference and note what is directly visible versus alleged. Editors should seek corroboration and a response from relevant parties where practicable. Do not submit private evidence or identifying details through public issue trackers.
