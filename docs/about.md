# About

This is an independent support project for civil society.

## What this is

- A public-interest resource supporting civil society.
- A way to document and responsibly share dated information about violence and rights concerns during or around protests.
- An autonomous project that makes missing evidence, verification and institutional responses easier to see.

## What this is not

- A political-party project, campaign platform or endorsement of any party, movement or individual.
- An official voice for any protest movement, its members, protesters or civil-society groups.
- A court, investigative authority, proof that every claim is verified or a final determination of responsibility.

The aim is practical: make gaps visible, help evidence be checked and preserved, and support safer, better-informed civic engagement.
