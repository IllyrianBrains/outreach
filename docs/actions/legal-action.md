# Legal action

Residents and environmental organizations have used several legal routes. A filing or investigation does not by itself establish wrongdoing.

- **[Legal-action summary — Citizens.al · 4 and 12 August 2026 ↗](https://citizens.al/en/2026/09/07/100-days-of-the-flamingo-revolution--institutions-under-citizen-pressure/)**  
  An overview of two complaints concerning development procedures in Vjosë–Nartë and other protected areas. Res Publica and EcoAlbania filed with the Vlorë Prosecutor’s Office on 4 August; a group of citizens filed separately with SPAK on 12 August. Neither filing establishes criminal responsibility.

- **[Vlorë airport appeal update — EuroNatur · 1 December 2025 ↗](https://www.euronatur.org/en/what-we-do/news/stage-victory-in-court-against-vlora-airport)**  
  Reports that the Administrative Court of Appeal recognized the environmental groups’ standing and returned the case to the first-instance court. This was a procedural decision, not a ruling on whether the airport is lawful.

- **[Bern Convention record · December 2025 ↗](https://rm.coe.int/misc-e-2025-45-standing-committee-clean-final/488029d956)**  
  The international treaty-monitoring record concerning environmental assessment, cumulative impacts and cooperation with civil society. Its recommendations add scrutiny but do not replace an Albanian court order.

- **[Vlorë airport lawsuit — PPNEA · 23 November 2022 ↗](https://ppnea.org/press-release-environmental-groups-suing-the-albanian-authorities-for-the-illegal-construction-of-the-vlora-airport/?lang=en)**  
  PPNEA and the Albanian Ornithological Society challenged administrative decisions enabling construction of Vlorë International Airport in the Vjosë–Nartë protected landscape.
