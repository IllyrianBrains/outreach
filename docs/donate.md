# Help equip students in Tirana

Small groups can pool money, agree on what is most urgent, and arrange one coordinated delivery of protective and first-aid equipment to Tirana. This page covers buying and shipping that equipment. For how to use it, know-your-rights information, and digital security, see the [protest safety guide](background/safety.md).

## Step 1 — Self-organize

Pick a small group, name one organizer, and use a single pool rather than several competing ones. Publish the target amount and who will receive the equipment in Tirana. The links below open each platform's official setup page, not an active campaign — replace them with your verified campaign link once the organizer creates one.

=== "GoFundMe"

    Best for a public fundraiser that can be shared widely.

    - Name the organizer and recipient.
    - Explain exactly what the money will buy.
    - Post receipts and delivery updates.

    [Set up GoFundMe ↗](https://www.gofundme.com/c/start){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

=== "PayPal Pool"

    Best for a defined private group making one shared purchase, where available and permitted.

    - Confirm that the purpose meets PayPal's terms.
    - Keep the pool separate from personal spending.
    - Share the final total and receipts with contributors.

    [Check PayPal Pool ↗](https://www.paypal.com/de/digital-wallet/send-receive-money/money-pools){ .md-button }

## Step 2 — Decide on priorities

Do not buy everything at once. Confirm quantities, sizes, and the most urgent category with trusted students in Tirana before spending anything.

=== "Medical toolkit"

    Buy complete sealed kits for trained first-aid volunteers. Do not distribute loose medication.

    | Buy | What to look for | Example |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Complete first-aid kit (DE: *Verbandkasten*) | Sterile dressings, gauze, gloves, scissors, triangular bandage, emergency blanket, **DIN 13164** (car/small kit) or **DIN 13157** (workplace kit, the EU/German standard size) | [DIN 13164 kit ↗](https://www.megateksa.com/sq/kuti-ndihma-e-shpejte-petex-din-13164-black) |
    | Larger team kit | Nitrile gloves, dressings, cleansing wipes, tape, shears, foil blanket | [ProSafety Albania ↗](https://www.prosafety.al/product/14) |
    | Sterile gauze refills (DE: *Mullkompressen*) | Individually sealed, multiple sizes, valid expiry date | [Pharmawest Albania ↗](https://pharmawest.al/products/pic-stericompress-swabs-12) |
    | Sterile saline (*ujë fiziologjik*; DE: *Kochsalzlösung* / *physiologische Kochsalzlösung*) | Sealed **0.9% sodium chloride**, single-use containers, Ph. Eur. (European Pharmacopoeia) grade, valid expiry date | Purchase from a pharmacy |
    | Eye-rinse supply | Sealed clean water and a suitable rinse bottle | [CDC guidance ↗](https://www.cdc.gov/chemical-emergencies/chemical-fact-sheets/riot-control-agents.html) |
    | Useful refills | Medical tape, cold packs, elastic bandages, non-latex gloves, emergency blankets | [Red Cross checklist ↗](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/anatomy-of-a-first-aid-kit.html) |

=== "Digital & legal support toolkit"

    These do not need delivery to Tirana — contributors can gift them directly or send money earmarked for legal aid.

    | Buy / support | What to look for | Example |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Power bank | 10,000+ mAh, multiple USB ports | Buy locally or ship compact |
    | Prepaid local SIM for a spare phone | Topped up, registered per Albanian rules, for a phone kept separate from a main device | Buy locally in Tirana |
    | Printed know-your-rights cards | Short, in Albanian, listing the right to remain silent and request a lawyer | Print locally; wording in the [protest safety guide](background/safety.md) |
    | Albanian Helsinki Committee — Free Legal Clinic | Donations support free legal defense for detained protesters | [AHC FAQ ↗](https://ahc.org.al/en/faq/) |
    | Signal (encrypted messaging app) | Free; no purchase needed — help contacts install and verify it | [Signal ↗](https://signal.org/) |

    Access Now's Digital Security Helpline is free and does not need funding to use. See the [protest safety guide](background/safety.md) for how to reach it and for phone-security steps worth doing before any money is spent.

A reminder before buying: a respirator only works when the mask fits and the filter matches the hazard, so don't buy one blindly, and no protective gear can make a confrontation safe. For riot-control-agent exposure, CDC guidance prioritizes leaving the area, fresh air, and rinsing irritated eyes with plain water. Call **112** for a serious injury, breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness, heavy bleeding, a suspected head or spinal injury, an eye injury, or a possible broken bone.

## Step 3 — Buy and ship

One coordinated local order is usually faster and cheaper than many separate diaspora parcels.

=== "Buy in Albania — preferred"

    - Order masks, gloves, first-aid kits, and gauze from Albanian suppliers.
    - Deliver directly to one verified recipient in Tirana.
    - Confirm stock and the final price before paying.
    - Keep the invoice and photograph the complete order.

    Megatek states that it accepts cards and PayPal and offers delivery within Albania. [Read its delivery information ↗](https://www.megateksa.com/sq/help-and-advice)

=== "Buy in Kosovo"

    - Useful for protective glasses and nearby work-safety suppliers.
    - Confirm who will transport the order from Kosovo to Tirana.
    - Avoid buying sized equipment until recipients confirm sizes.

=== "Buy in Germany / EU"

    - Best for compact standardized first-aid kits or size-confirmed protection.
    - Compare the delivered cost with buying the same category in Albania.
    - Check expiry dates, courier or airline restrictions, and customs before paying.

    Examples: [DIN 13157 kit ↗](https://www.shop-apotheke.com/sanitaetshaus/upmUPFEDP/pvs-medic-1-erste-hilfe-set-din-13157.htm) · [DIN 13164 kit ↗](https://www.shop-apotheke.com/sanitaetshaus/upmZLTZZW/pvs-eurokit-din-13164-2022.htm)

## Close the loop

After the order, contributors should receive:

1. The final amount collected.
2. The shopping list and receipts.
3. The remaining balance.
4. A delivery confirmation that does not expose recipients.

Prices and availability change. Product examples were checked on 19 October 2026 and are not endorsements.
