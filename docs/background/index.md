# Background

The protests began around opposition to development affecting the **Vjosë–Nartë protected coastal landscape**. The Flamingo Revolution says the movement later expanded to education, public services, democratic representation, accountability and the rule of law.

!!! info "Origins, events and documentation"
    This section brings together movement context, a dated timeline, academic-protest mapping, incident evidence and the sources used across the site.

## Scope

!!! info "How to read this section"
    This section provides context without endorsing every claim or demand. It distinguishes movement claims, media reporting, institutional statements and independently established findings. Inclusion does not prove wrongdoing, and the actions of one participant should not be attributed to an entire group.
