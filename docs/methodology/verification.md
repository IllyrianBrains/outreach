# Verification, neutrality and safeguarding

## Source classification

- **Primary record:** original video, official document, direct testimony; authenticity and context still require checks.
- **Attributed report:** an identifiable outlet or institution reports a claim; retain the attribution.
- **Allegation:** a person or group asserts a fact that has not been independently established.
- **Verified finding:** supported by sufficiently reliable independent evidence or a competent investigative finding; document methodology.

## Before publication

1. Record source title, publisher, URL, publication time, capture time if known, and archive reference.
2. Check whether footage is original, complete, correctly dated and geolocated; do not infer unseen events.
3. Seek statements from authorities, injured persons and witnesses without pressuring them.
4. Separate counts of injured, arrested, detained and taken for questioning; record provenance and timestamps.
5. Verify quotations against the original language; label translations.
6. Redact unnecessary names, faces, medical information and details identifying minors.
7. Document any correction with a timestamp and changed wording.
8. Do not claim an NGO statement is authenticated solely from a social media link.

## Outreach process

Maintain a restricted contact register with organization, role, country, public contact channel, relevance, date sent, reply and next action. Do not publish personal email addresses, track political views of individuals, or mass-message recipients without regard to relevance. Use one carefully tailored follow-up and honor requests not to be contacted.

## Editorial independence

Avoid claims that an entire assembly was violent or peaceful based on isolated footage. Do not treat police disciplinary action as proof of criminal guilt. Request accountability for alleged violations by any party and offer meaningful opportunity for response.

## Update log and corrections

For each substantive change, record **date/time (with timezone), editor, changed claim, supporting source, and reason**. Correct inaccurate claims visibly, rather than silently replacing them. If a source is inaccessible, mark it as unconfirmed and do not quote it as verified.
