
# 🇪🇸 Spain

**Country guide · 15 October 2026**

Use this page to share a factual, source-linked briefing about the protests in Albania. This is a public guide, not a list of endorsed politicians or organizations.

## 1. Choose who to contact

- **Media:** national or local news desks, foreign-affairs editors, or journalists covering Albania and the Western Balkans. Ask for fact-based coverage.
- **Elected representatives:** your constituency representative or the parliamentary foreign affairs/human rights committee. Ask for an inquiry to the responsible ministry, within their powers.
- **Civil society:** independent human rights, journalism, student or legal-aid organizations. Ask them to assess the evidence and consider monitoring or referrals.

## 3. Attach evidence and follow up

Use the [latest briefing](../briefs/tirana-2026-10-13-15-urgent.md) and link to original reporting. Confirm the recipient’s role and contact details from their official website. Keep a private record of date sent, recipient and response.

!!! tip "Appropriate requests"
    Ask for protection of peaceful assembly and journalists, evidence preservation, independent investigation of alleged abuses by any actor, and a written response. Avoid unsupported allegations or party-political endorsements.
