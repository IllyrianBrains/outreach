# Evidence comparison

Compare numerical claims and source notes without treating repeated reporting as independent confirmation. Entries come from the workbook's **Evidence (numbers and source)** field.

!!! warning "How to compare responsibly"
    Different figures may cover different dates, locations, groups or definitions. An arrest count is not an injury count; an incident is not necessarily one affected person; and syndicated reports may repeat the same underlying source. Preserve attribution and do not add figures unless their scopes are demonstrably separate.

## Reading conflicts

When two figures appear inconsistent, check:

1. **Period:** single day, several days, or cumulative through publication.
2. **Population:** protesters, police, bystanders, journalists, minors, detainees or all people contacted.
3. **Unit:** people, incidents, complaints, arrests, criminal proceedings or evidence items.
4. **Attribution:** direct institutional figure, civil-society monitoring, media reporting or republished material.
5. **Status:** preliminary, alleged, independently verified, corrected or final.
