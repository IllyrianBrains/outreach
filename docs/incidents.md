# Incident and harm records

This registry covers **all affected groups**, including demonstrators, students, bystanders, journalists, police and detainees. Records can include violence, threats, interference and property damage. **An entry is not a finding of wrongdoing.**

## How to contribute

Add one record per distinct incident where possible. Use `source_ids` to reference `sources.csv` (separate multiple IDs with semicolons). Preserve uncertainties and note if a count is an aggregate rather than an individual case. Avoid identifying minors, patients, or witnesses without consent. Do not publish private medical or custody records.

## Relationship to ADCN

The **Albanian Diaspora Civic Network (ADCN)** published a four-page public-source review dated 14 October 2026 concerning the 13 October Tirana protest. It includes visual analysis (pages 1–2), cross-source reporting (page 3) and preservation/investigation requests (page 4). The `adcn_reference` field points to relevant pages. These records are a **new organizational layer**, not an ADCN investigation or a replacement for its original publication.
