# Actions

Public initiatives connected to Vjosë–Nartë and the wider Flamingo Revolution.

- **[Open letters](open-letters.md)**  
  Published appeals and statements from environmental and diaspora organizations.

- **[Civic projects](civic-projects.md)**  
  Public tools for participation, documentation, mapping and diaspora coordination.

- **[Referendums and citizen initiatives](referendums.md)**  
  Signature campaigns seeking repeal referendums or proposing changes to Albanian law.

- **[Legal action](legal-action.md)**  
  Complaints, court cases and international procedures concerning protected areas and development projects.

- **[Moratorium](moratorium.md)**  
  Public and institutional calls to pause development in protected areas.

- **[Funding and support](funding.md)**  
  Available disclosure records for conservation organizations and civic support.

!!! note
    A complaint is not a finding, a signature campaign is not a completed referendum, and an institutional resolution is not automatically national law.
