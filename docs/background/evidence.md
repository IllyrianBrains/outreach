# Evidence

Browse sourced reports of incidents affecting protesters, students, bystanders, journalists, police and detainees. A listed incident is not a finding of wrongdoing.

## Evidence documents

The following documents have different dates, methods and scopes. Their counts should not be added together or treated as final findings.

| Document | Scope and reported figures | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ADCN public-source evidence brief, 14 October 2026](../assets/evidence/ADCN_Tirana_13_October_2026_Evidence_Brief.pdf) | Four-page review of reporting and visual material from **13 October in Tirana**. It records reported use of tear gas and water cannon, a reported serious leg injury, restraint images, injuries and detentions; it also identifies claims the authors could not verify. | Public secondary compilation; follow its links to the original publishers. |
| [Qendra Res Publica open letter and annex, 17 October 2026](../assets/evidence/Leter-e-hapur-Policia-e-Shtetit-dhuna-13-17-tetor-2026.pdf) | Catalogues **32 numbered cases** reported from **13–17 October**. Citing the Ombudsman's preliminary findings, it says monitors met **214 detained people** and recorded visible signs of violence or mistreatment allegations for **26** of them. It also reports about **12 minors** were being checked as possibly held in isolation. | Civil-society submission containing attributed allegations and requests for investigation; not a judicial or final investigative finding. |
| [Lawyers' cover letter to Amnesty International, 20 October 2026](../assets/evidence/Amnesty_Cover_Letter_Group.pdf) | Says an enclosed confidential report reviewed **124 items** covering **13–20 October**, including **31 entries numbered 94–124** that had not previously been shared. It describes protests in Tirana and **at least eight other towns** and asks for an urgent research mission. | The public file is a request and cover letter. The referenced report, visual annex and confidential evidence log are **not included here**, so their contents cannot be independently checked from this repository. |
| [Albanian Ombudsman monitoring statement, 20 October 2026](../assets/evidence/Avokati%20i%20popullit.pdf) | A **24-hour monitoring snapshot** associated with the 19 October protest: **50** people taken to police stations, including **18 minors**; **109** people found in the Security Block; **193** people contacted across detention and hospital settings; and **11 complaints** recorded. | Official preliminary monitoring statement. It does not say that all 109 people in the Security Block were detained at the 19 October protest. Its hospital total is 34, while the listed groups add up to 33. |

These are document-level figures: a "case," an "item," a person met in detention and a complaint are not equivalent units. Later submissions may also include earlier material.

## Recording standard

!!! tip "How records are handled"
    Keep every report linked to its source. Distinguish observations, allegations and established findings; preserve uncertainty and verification limits; protect minors, witnesses and private medical information; and never infer responsibility from an image or report alone.
