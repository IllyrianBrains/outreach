# Referendums and citizen initiatives

Several signature campaigns were active in 2026. Collecting signatures begins a legal process; it does not automatically schedule a vote or enact a bill.

- **[KQZ acts register · ongoing; checked 18 October 2026 ↗](https://kqz.gov.al/akte)**  
  The official register for decisions, extensions and other procedural updates. Use it to confirm whether a collection period or campaign status has changed.

- **[Protected Areas referendum dossier · collection began 24 August 2026 ↗](https://referendum21.org/rreth-nesh)**  
  A campaign seeking repeal of the 2024 amendments to Albania’s Protected Areas law, rather than repeal of the entire protected-areas framework. It requires at least 50,000 eligible-voter signatures.

- **[KQZ approval for the Protected Areas campaign · 20 August 2026 ↗](https://www.kqz.gov.al/lajme/kshz-miraton-fleten-tip-per-mbledhjen-e-nenshkrimeve-per-referendum-te-pergjithshem)**  
  The official election authority’s notice approving the standard signature form. Approval allows signature collection to proceed; it does not mean that a referendum has already been scheduled.

- **[Electoral Code citizen initiative — KQZ · 15 June 2026 ↗](https://www.kqz.gov.al/lajme/kshz-miraton-fleten-tip-per-mbledhjen-e-nenshkrimeve-per-nismen-ligjvenese-per-projektligjin-per-shtesa-e-ndryshime-ne-kodin-zgjedhor)**  
  A proposed citizen bill addressing matters including open lists, election administration and political-finance controls. It requires 20,000 signatures and proceeds to Parliament if validated; it is not a referendum.

- **[Mountains Package and Strategic Investments campaign · 7 May 2026 ↗](https://referendum.shqiperiabehet.al/)**  
  Two separate campaigns seeking repeal referendums for Law 20/2025 and Law 55/2015. KQZ approved their standard signature forms on 7 May; each campaign requires 50,000 signatures and uses a separate form.
